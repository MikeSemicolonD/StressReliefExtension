Physics Settings

Default behavior is a top-down view (no gravity). Raise gravity if you want elements to fall.

Shake Detection Settings

A swing is one push of the window in a direction before you reverse it. Lower the distance or required swings if shaking doesn't trigger physics.

Grab the window by its title bar and shake it to start, or click the extension icon. While physics is on, moving the window sloshes everything around. Press Esc or click the icon again to put the page back.
